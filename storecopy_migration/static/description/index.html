StoreCopy Migration

Move your store to or from Odoo in one click

StoreCopy copies your catalog, customers, orders and coupon codes between Odoo and Shopify, HubSpot, Wix, WooCommerce, Squarespace, PrestaShop, Magento, BigCommerce and more than 20 other platforms.

  • Products and variants: names, descriptions, SKUs, barcodes, prices, costs, weights, attributes, tags, images, stock and website SEO.
  • Categories with their hierarchy.
  • Customers: names, emails, phones, addresses, tags and notes.
  • Orders: lines linked to the migrated products and customers, totals, taxes, billing and shipping addresses, notes.
  • Coupons: loyalty coupon codes with their discount.

How it works

  1. Install the app and open StoreCopy from the main menu.
  2. Choose the direction and click Connect to StoreCopy. An API key named "StoreCopy migration" is created for your user.
  3. Connect the other platform on storecopy.com, review the free estimate and start the migration.
  4. Click Disconnect when you are done: the key is deleted.

When Odoo is the source, StoreCopy only reads. Your Odoo must be reachable from the internet (Odoo.sh or your own server). Questions: support@storecopy.com.
