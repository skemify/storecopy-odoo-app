StoreCopy Migration

Move your store or CRM to or from Odoo, without passwords or API keys

Watch the demo: Odoo → Shopify, a free demo migration step by step (2:30)

StoreCopy copies your e-commerce data (catalog, customers, orders and coupon codes) between Odoo and Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Square Online, PrestaShop, Magento, Shopware, OpenCart, GoDaddy and Tiendanube, among other e-commerce platforms.

  • Products and variants: names, descriptions, SKUs, barcodes, prices, weights, attributes, tags, images, stock and website SEO.
  • Categories with their hierarchy.
  • Customers: names, emails, phones, addresses, tags and notes.
  • Orders: lines linked to the migrated products and customers, totals, taxes, billing and shipping addresses, notes.
  • Coupons: loyalty coupon codes with their discount (needs the Odoo Discount & Loyalty app).

CRM: companies, contacts, products, leads, opportunities, quotations, orders, invoices and activities from Odoo to HubSpot and Zoho CRM; companies, contacts, products, leads, opportunities and activities to Pipedrive; companies, contacts, deals, orders and activities to ActiveCampaign; and from Zoho CRM or Pipedrive into Odoo. The app needs no Sales or Inventory app: storecopy.com tells you which Odoo apps a migration needs before it starts.

How it works

  1. Install the app.
  2. On storecopy.com, choose Odoo and enter your Odoo address. Sign in to Odoo if asked and click Allow: an API key named "StoreCopy migration" is created for your user and handed to StoreCopy. No password or key to copy. (Or open StoreCopy in Odoo and click Connect to StoreCopy.)
  3. Connect the other platform, review the free estimate and start the migration.
  4. Click Disconnect in the StoreCopy menu when you are done: the key is deleted.

When Odoo is the source, StoreCopy only reads. Your Odoo must be reachable from the internet over https (Odoo.sh or your own server). Odoo Online can't install third-party apps; on its Custom plan, which includes the External API, connect on storecopy.com with an API key instead. Only Odoo administrators (Settings access) can connect. Questions: support@storecopy.com.

How it looks

In Odoo, open StoreCopy, choose the direction and click Connect to StoreCopy.

StoreCopy creates an API key for your user and opens the migration wizard.

On storecopy.com both stores are connected: Odoo as the source, Shopify as the destination.

The free estimate counts what will be copied and shows the price.

Run a free demo migration (up to 20 items per category) and roll it back with one click.

Products arrive with descriptions, images, tags, prices and their size and color variants.
